About Simple Macro Guide

Clear macro education for everyday readers in the United Kingdom and the United States.

Simple Macro Guide exists to make core macroeconomics readable without hype. We explain prices, jobs, growth, policy, and trade in calm language, with links to primary data providers such as FRED, ONS, BEA, BLS, the IMF, and the World Bank.

Who publishes this site

Simple Macro Guide publishes these pages for education. It is not a broker, adviser, or product pitch page. The operator is identified in the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy, and the disclaimer. For messages, use Contact or email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com with the subject line [Contact: simplemacroguide].

Mission

Help readers build a steady map of macro ideas so headlines are less confusing. We favour definitions, mechanisms, and source links over forecasts or trade ideas. Soft next steps on the site point to FAQ, the data guide, and Contact when you want to reach us.

Editorial standards

  • Original writing. Explainers and topic pages are written for this site. We do not scrape competitor articles.
  • Primary sources. Claims about data point to official or widely used research outlets. We avoid invented statistics.
  • Educational framing. Content is not investment, financial, tax, or legal advice. See the disclaimer.
  • UK and US lens. Where institutions differ, we say so instead of pretending one country’s label fits all.
  • Corrections. If something is wrong, email us. We prefer fixing pages over defending mistakes.

What we do not do (yet)

Educational quality comes first. The site shows Google advertising, kept clearly separate from the lessons, and has no affiliate links. Trust and clarity come first.
