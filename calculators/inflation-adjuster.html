Inflation adjuster

Estimate what an amount in one year is worth in another year, assuming a constant average annual inflation rate. Runs entirely offline in your browser.

How it works. Adjusted ≈ Amount × (1 + rate/100)(to − from). You can project forward or backward by swapping years. For historical accuracy, use year-by-year official index ratios from BLS CPI or ONS instead of a single average rate.
Example defaults

Defaults ($100 from 2000 to 2024 at 2.5%) are illustrative. They are not a claim about actual US or UK inflation over that span.
