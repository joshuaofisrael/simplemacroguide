Image policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains how Simple Macro Guide uses pictures. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC publishes the site. Questions about an image can be sent through the contact page.

AI generated photographs

The photographs and photo-style illustrations on this site are AI generated images. They are not photographs of real classrooms, offices, or public events, and they are not pictures of real people as they appeared. They are decorative illustrations that sit beside the explainers. A caption under each of these images says that it is an illustrative AI generated photo and includes a Source link to this page.

Teaching sketches

Some explainers also use teaching sketches: simple diagrams drawn to show a relationship such as supply and demand or a business cycle. Those sketches are labelled as teaching sketches. They are not photographs, they are not drawn to a real dataset unless the caption says otherwise, and they are not AI generated photos.

Credits and questions

If a licensed or Creative Commons photograph is added later, its caption will name the source. Until then, treat every photo-style image on this site as an AI generated illustration and use this page as the source credit. If you have a question about an image, use the contact page.
