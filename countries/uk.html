United Kingdom

Country snapshot · Educational orientation · Verify live figures at the source

The United Kingdom is a services-intensive open economy with London as a major financial centre. Macro releases from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) and policy from the Bank of England (Bank Rate and Monetary Policy Committee communications) dominate the domestic calendar. Sterling floats against other major currencies.

Where to look up numbers

  • Office for National Statistics (ONS) — GDP, CPI/CPIH, labour market, trade.
  • Bank of England — Bank Rate, Monetary Policy Reports, financial stability.
  • HM Treasury — fiscal policy and Budget documents.
  • World Bank — United Kingdom — internationally comparable series.
  • FRED also hosts selected UK series useful for cross-checks.

Institutions at a glance

AreaPrimary institution
Monetary policyBank of England (MPC)

Example (not live data)

UK inflation commentary often distinguishes CPI and CPIH; housing measurement differs from US CPI shelter treatment. For the latest annual rate, use the ONS inflation release rather than any figure remembered from news cycles. Start at the ONS inflation hub.

Themes for readers

Mortgage markets with a sizable share of floating or near-term fixed deals can transmit Bank Rate changes relatively quickly to households. Trade and migration arrangements after Brexit remain part of the structural backdrop. Public debt and gilt markets are central to fiscal–monetary interaction.
