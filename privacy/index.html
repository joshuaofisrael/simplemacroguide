Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information simplemacroguide.com collects and how it is used. The short version: no accounts, no advertising, no affiliate links, no tracking cookies, and we never sell personal information.

Who is responsible for your information

Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the data controller for personal information processed through this website. Simple Macro Guide is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, not a separate company. You can reach us at joshuaofisrael@gmail.com.

What we collect

Emails you send us

The form on our contact page does not send anything to our servers or to any form service. When you press send, it opens a new message in your own email app, addressed to joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. We only receive what you choose to send by email, usually your name, your email address and your message. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages on our behalf.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

Each page loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy focused analytics script, so we can count visits and see how quickly pages load. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or local storage and does not track individual visitors across sites. It records information such as the page viewed, the referring site, browser and device type, approximate country and page load timings. We only see aggregate reports. We cannot identify you from them. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your IP address when the script loads.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses when it serves pages, for security and to keep the service running. We do not have access to those logs.

Calculators

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent to us or stored.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or sign ups.
  • No advertising networks, such as Google AdSense.
  • No affiliate links or affiliate tracking.
  • No Google Analytics, social media pixels or other marketing trackers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies, and Cloudflare Web Analytics works without them. If you follow a link to another website, that site has its own cookie and privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To read and reply to messages you send, including corrections and permission requests.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve them.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

If UK or EU data protection law applies to you, we rely on our legitimate interests in running and improving an educational website and in answering messages that you choose to send us.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers named above (Google for email, Cloudflare for analytics and GitHub for hosting), or where the law requires it. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need to deal with your message and keep reasonable records, and we delete them sooner if you ask. Analytics data is held by Cloudflare under its own retention rules, and we only view it in aggregate.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. To make a request, email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. If you are in the EU, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, for example by adding advertising in future, we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.

See also our Terms of Use and Disclaimer.
